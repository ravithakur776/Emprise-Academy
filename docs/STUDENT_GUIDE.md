# Emprise Academy — Student & Parent Guide

---

## 1. Accessing Your Student Portal
Students enrolled at Emprise Academy or registered for the Emprise Talent Search Examination (ETSE) can access their personal portal at:
👉 **[https://empriseacademy.com/student/login](https://empriseacademy.com/student/login)**

---

## 2. ETSE 2026 Registration & Admit Card Pass
1. Register for ETSE 2026 at `https://empriseacademy.com/etse-2026`.
2. Upon submission, you will receive a unique Application Number (e.g. `ETSE2026-000100`).
3. Your Admit Card with Roll Number, Exam Date (**6 September 2026**), and Exam Centre address will be accessible under **My Admit Cards** (`/student/admit-cards`).
4. Print your admit card and carry it to your assigned exam venue.

---

## 3. Checking Examination Results
- **Public Search**: Enter your Roll Number and Date of Birth at `https://empriseacademy.com/results` to view your verified scorecard.
- **Student Account**: Log in to `/student/results` to view your comprehensive subject-wise breakdown, percentile, rank, and scholarship eligibility certificate.

---

## 4. Password Recovery
If you forget your password, click **Forgot Password?** on the login screen or navigate to `/student/forgot-password`. Enter your registered email address to receive a secure reset link.
